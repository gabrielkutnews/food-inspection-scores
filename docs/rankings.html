Austin-area restaurants, ranked by inspection consistency

Average score across every inspection on record — not a snapshot of a single visit. Restaurants, cafes and coffee shops only, each with an inspection in the last 18 months.

Best records

Highest average, among restaurants inspected at least 5 times. A spotless record over three visits is common enough to be unremarkable, so the bar is higher here than for the list below.

Lowest average scores

Lowest average, among restaurants inspected at least 3 times. A low average across three visits is already a pattern rather than one bad day, which is why the threshold is lower — requiring five would hide the worst-scoring restaurants in the city.

How this was built. One row per licensed restaurant, averaged across every inspection on record. Excludes five facilities scored 0 — a 0 means no score was issued, not a failing kitchen — plus names carrying an out-of-business or ineligible-for-renewal flag. Where one operator licenses several counters at a single address, only its best-scoring counter appears. Chains and coffee shops count as restaurants; schools, groceries, convenience stores, care facilities, stadium concessions, staff-only canteens and retailers that merely hold a food permit are excluded. The city publishes no facility-type field, so type is inferred from the establishment name and a few will be misclassified — each list was checked by hand before publication. Source: City of Austin food establishment inspection scores. Generated August 3, 2026.
